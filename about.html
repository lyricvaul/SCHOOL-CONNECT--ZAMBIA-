About SchoolConnect

SchoolConnect is a comprehensive digital transformation platform designed specifically for the Zambian education sector. Our mission is to bridge the gap between educational institutions, educators, students, and parents through seamless technology.

Our Vision

To be the central nervous system of education in Zambia, from the smallest primary school in Luapula to the largest university in Lusaka.

Unified Access

One platform for all institutional levels: Primary, Secondary, Colleges, and Universities.

Local Context

Tailored pricing and features that reflect the needs and financial realities of Zambian education.

Platform Capabilities

  • Secure Multi-Institution Support
  • Role-Based Access Control (6 Unique Roles)
  • Digital Lesson Content Creation (Video & Text)
  • Automated Attendance & Results Tracking
  • Mobile-Optimized Experience for Parents and Students

Note: This website is a demonstration of the SchoolConnect platform concepts. Technical features like secure login and payment processing are architectural placeholders in this version.
